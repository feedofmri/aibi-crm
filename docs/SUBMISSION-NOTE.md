# BIAI CRM — submission note

**What I built.** An admissions CRM for BIAI on the 20 supplied messages, which become 19 contacts (Rafi’s two messages stay on one contact). Pasting a message extracts the name, phone, programme, queue (Sales, Support, Careers or Spam) and language, and drafts a reply in Bangla, Banglish or English. A person reviews the result before saving. Contacts move through New → Contacted → Interested → Paid → Lost on a drag-and-drop board, with WhatsApp and call links, an activity history showing who changed what, a 48-hour follow-up queue and a weekly brief. An optional Automatic mode saves complete sales enquiries and holds exceptions for review. Data is stored in PostgreSQL, with a SQLite fallback so the app runs with one command.

I made the judgement calls: a payment claim never marks a contact Paid; an unclear programme stays Unspecified; replies never invent prices, dates or venues; code, not the model, calculates the weekly totals.

**What doesn’t work yet.** Team sign-in records who made each change but doesn’t yet restrict access. The app doesn’t send messages, verify payments or connect to WhatsApp or Facebook directly. Reminders are in-app only. Without an AI key, new messages use a weaker, clearly labelled rule-based fallback. The 20/20 extraction match comes from the same samples I tuned the prompts on.

**What I’d fix next.** Enforce sign-in and roles on the API, add approved programme and fee information so replies can actually answer, then add a reviewed WhatsApp integration.
