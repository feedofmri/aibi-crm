# Brand notes

Both sites follow the light theme of the BIAI website ([biai.com.bd](https://biai.com.bd/)), adapted to a compact working interface.

- **Colours:** warm paper `#FAF7F2`, white cards, ink `#0D0D0D`, primary orange `#C4511A`.
- **Type:** Inter for English and Hind Siliguri for Bangla, the same families as the website. Headings use a regular weight with tight letter spacing, and section labels are small orange uppercase "eyebrows".
- **Components:** orange pill-shaped buttons, rounded white cards, thin neutral borders, restrained shadows and generous spacing.
- **Restraint:** no hero images, background video, dark dashboard or decorative marketing elements.

The fonts are stored locally, so the app doesn't depend on a font CDN. Their licences (SIL Open Font License, Inter licence) are in `client/src/assets/fonts/`.

The BIAI icon and wordmark are local copies of the assets on the official site, and they belong to BIAI. The website was used as a visual reference only. No course prices, policies or marketing copy from it were added to the CRM's knowledge.
