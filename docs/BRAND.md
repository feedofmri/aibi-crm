# BIAI visual references

The CRM uses the light theme from BIAI’s Courses/About pages, as requested in the supplied screenshots, adapted to a compact operational interface.

- Paper: `#FAF7F2`; white cards; ink: `#0D0D0D`; primary orange: `#C4511A`.
- Inter for English, Hind Siliguri for Bangla, matching the official website’s font families. Font files are local so the demo does not depend on a font CDN. Their SIL Open Font Licenses are included in `public/assets/`.
- Orange pill-shaped actions, rounded white cards, restrained shadows, thin neutral borders and generous spacing.
- No cinematic hero, background video, dark dashboard or decorative marketing imagery.
- The original BIAI icon and black wordmark are local copies of the assets served by the official site. These marks belong to BIAI and are used only for this BIAI hiring prototype; no affiliation beyond the task is implied.

Sources inspected 3 November 2026: [BIAI website](https://biai.com.bd/), [Courses](https://biai.com.bd/courses), [About](https://biai.com.bd/about). Logo sources: `https://biai.com.bd/baai-icon.png` and `https://biai.com.bd/baai-logo.png`. Fonts originate from the site’s own font declarations; licenses from the Inter project and Google Fonts’ Hind Siliguri distribution.

The website and screenshots are visual references only. Current course prices, policies and programme marketing have not been added as CRM knowledge: the task requires use of only the supplied enquiry data.

The final interface uses plain operational headings, compact statistics, restrained corners, neutral surfaces and minimal decoration. Promotional slogans, decorative sparkles and the repeated live-AI banner were removed. Draft provenance remains visible in the review workflow.

UX review: increased text contrast and control sizes; 240px minimum board columns with a List alternative; reset filters; mobile scrolling navigation; grouped contact fields and conditional support controls; pending reviews sorted first with expandable forms; overview metrics hidden on reporting and automation pages. Preserved the BIAI palette and four-point star on AI actions.
